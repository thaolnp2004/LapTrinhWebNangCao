## Prompt logo

Use case: logo-brand. Create a refined professional cosmetics boutique logo for the exact brand text "SUN COSMETIC". A delicate rose pink sun motif whose rays look like soft petals, with an elegant tiny golden center. Horizontal lockup: small round sun icon at left, the words SUN COSMETIC in a beautiful readable modern luxury serif at right, with SUN slightly larger. Palette deep rose #b63b64 and warm gold #c99c63. Simple flat vector-like clean lines, polished contemporary beauty identity, excellent readability at website header size. Transparent background with true alpha, no mockup, no paper texture, no shadows, no slogan, no extra words. Wide horizontal aspect ratio.
